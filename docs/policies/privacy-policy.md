**PRIVACY POLICY**  
Seek by Ritu  
Effective date: November 3, 2026

**1. Who We Are**

- Seek by Ritu is operated by Rituparna Das, operating as BLINDSPOT STUDIOS in Calgary, Alberta, Canada.
- This policy covers personal information collected through our program registration, questionnaires, CRM and related communications.
- **Privacy contact:** Rituparna Das, Owner and Privacy Contact.
- **Email:** seekbyritu@gmail.com.

**2. Information We Collect**

Depending on your interactions with us, we collect:

- Your name, email, phone number, country and time zone.
- Your selected program, agreement, signature and payment status.
- Questionnaire answers, goals, habits, daily check-ins, journal entries and coaching notes.
- Information you choose to share about Health, Relationships, Career, Money and Spirituality.
- Account activity and technical information needed to operate, troubleshoot and protect the CRM.

Share only information relevant to your program. Avoid identifying other people or uploading unnecessary sensitive documents.

**3. How We Use Your Information**

We use your information to:

- Register you and manage your account.
- Create your personalized plan, habits and resources.
- Review progress, schedule calls and provide support.
- Process invoices, verify payments and assess deposit-refund eligibility.
- Maintain business records, protect our systems and meet legal obligations.

We request separate permission before using identifiable testimonials, screenshots, photographs or personal stories for marketing.

**4. Consent and Your Choices**

- We explain why information is needed when collecting it and obtain consent where required.
- Sensitive coaching information is collected with your explicit consent, unless another lawful basis applies.
- You may decline optional information or contact us to withdraw consent, subject to legal or contractual limits.
- If essential information is unavailable, we will explain how this affects our ability to provide your program.
- Marketing consent is separate from program participation.

**5. Service Providers and Payments**

- We use service providers for CRM hosting, payment processing and related business operations.
- Render hosts our CRM in **Oregon, United States (US West)**.
- Stripe processes card payments for applicable packages. Your bank processes e-Transfers.
- Do not enter card numbers, banking passwords or other payment credentials into questionnaires, journals or messages.
- We limit information provided to service providers to what is reasonably needed for their work.
- We do not sell or rent your personal information.
- We may disclose information with your consent or where permitted or required by law.

**6. Information Outside Canada**

- Information submitted to our CRM is processed through hosting in **Oregon, USA** to operate the CRM and deliver your program.
- Information processed outside Canada is subject to the laws of the relevant country and may be accessible to its courts, law enforcement or other authorities.
- Other providers may process information in additional locations. Contact us for current details about our providers, processing locations and related practices.
- Questions about foreign processing may be directed to **Rituparna Das at seekbyritu@gmail.com**.

**7. Security**

- We take reasonable administrative, technical and physical measures to protect personal information.
- Access is limited to people and service providers who need it for authorized purposes.
- No online system is completely secure. Keep your login details private and report suspected unauthorized access promptly.
- We assess privacy incidents and notify affected individuals and regulators where legally required.

**8. Retention and Deletion**

- We retain personal information only as long as reasonably needed for program delivery, legitimate business purposes and legal obligations.
- Financial, agreement and dispute-related records may need to be retained after your program ends.
- When information is no longer required, we securely delete it or make it non-identifiable.
- Backup copies may remain until overwritten under the provider’s backup cycle.
- Contact us to request deletion or ask about retention periods for specific records.

**9. Access, Corrections and Complaints**

- You may request access to your personal information, corrections or information about its use and disclosure.
- You may also request deletion or withdrawal of consent, subject to applicable limits.
- We may verify your identity before acting on a request.
- We respond within applicable legal timelines and explain any lawful refusal or limitation.
- Send requests or complaints to **seekbyritu@gmail.com**.
- If your concern remains unresolved, you may contact the **Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta**, or another privacy regulator with jurisdiction.

**10. Policy Updates**

- We update this policy when our practices change.
- The current version will show its effective date.
- We will provide additional notice and obtain fresh consent where required for material changes.
