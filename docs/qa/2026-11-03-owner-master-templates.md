# Owner-approved universal document system

The Canada agreement and deposit-invoice PDFs supplied by the owner define the shared layout: original logo, green top border, Times title, Helvetica body, numbered green sections, bullet details, restrained totals bands, footer and page count. The active invoice/agreement/receipt renderer now uses this visual system, including arbitrary future catalogue offers and customised invoices. The owner's original signature and its original transparency mask were extracted losslessly from the agreement. It is included in agreement PDFs; there is no public standalone image route. Client signatures are never fabricated.

All three supplied offers have complete package-specific defaults in public/programme-content.js. Canada uses CAD 150 + 350 by Interac e-Transfer to seekbyritu@gmail.com, 10 coaching calls, a 15-minute plan review, 95% deposit eligibility and five-business-day deposit return after completion. India Guided uses CAD 100 through the supplied Stripe link and six 20-minute calls. India Self-Guided uses CAD 50 through its supplied Stripe link and no private calls or walkthrough. Clauses are adapted only where the owner supplied applicable content; Canada deposit benefits are not copied into India products.

The latest explicit timing instructions supersede the earlier Canada reference wording: plan preparation is 3–5 business days for every package; Canada plan review is 15 minutes; Canada access/start is scheduled within one business day after verified balance and completed onboarding. Existing active programme dates remain unchanged. Business-day arithmetic excludes Saturday/Sunday; statutory-holiday handling is not configured.

First-step emails include the relevant agreement, invoice, actual payment instructions and a clearly numbered What Happens Next section. Product defaults expose editable next-step instructions; unsent programme drafts read those defaults, while generic Billing snapshots support customer-specific instructions. Sent messages are preserved. Failed programme email preparations are rebuilt so they do not retain stale attachments. No real message or payment was sent during development.

New invoice references are random, alphabetical and non-sequential, with IC-CA, IC-IG and IC-IS prefixes (SBR for other offers). Receipt records link to the original invoice reference. Existing issued references and financial records are not renumbered. Selected issue/due/coach-agreement dates and customer phone details flow into PDFs. Native date fields have visible Calendar buttons; actual client acknowledgement dates come from the recorded acknowledgement timestamp.

A one-time production migration installs the owner's approved terms, descriptions, next steps and formatting for the three standard services. Previous catalogue versions are retained encrypted in billing_catalogue_history. Previously issued/signed records remain intact; mismatched new formatting cannot override historical accepted text. Later coach edits are not overwritten by the migration.

Verification: 50 regression tests passed. Seven final targeted browser/workflow/migration tests also passed after the UI labels were aligned. All six package samples were visually inspected and checked for expected page counts: one page per invoice and two pages per agreement, nine pages combined. Original signature presence and personalised acknowledged-agreement pagination were verified. A historical-agreement check confirmed old accepted wording takes precedence over unrelated new formatting. Syntax checks pass for 69 JavaScript files. These results do not establish that Render has deployed the commit or prove actual Gmail inbox delivery.
