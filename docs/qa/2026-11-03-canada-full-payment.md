# Canada optional full payment

Canada Premium supports an owner-selected CAD 500 full payment alongside its default CAD 150 deposit + CAD 350 balance. Select it at registration or in Client Overview → Payments & Documents before any payment is verified. The choice locks after verification. India packages and catalogue prices, agreement/refund terms, call entitlements and standard instalment workflow remain unchanged.

The full-payment flow provides one CAD 500 invoice and, after manual verification of the payment date, method and transaction reference, one CAD 500 paid-in-full receipt. The service description is “Inner Code: Personalised Health, Wellness & Self-Mastery Coaching”. Refund conditions remain in the agreement, not the receipt. Neither document promises reimbursement or medical eligibility.

Full payment unlocks the private questionnaire but does not bypass questionnaire submission, plan preparation, review and approval. A separate activation message follows readiness; no CAD 350 payment request is created. No payment processor, external email or live Render database was exercised during tests.

Validation: 55 tests passed, including browser registration, full-payment verification, unpaid receipt rejection and PDF retrieval, plus readiness/activation and existing package regressions. Invoice and receipt previews were generated and inspected; each is one page. Deployment to Render has not been independently verified.
